Detroit Cafes

My personal ratings and notes for cafes around Detroit. Updated as I visit new places.

Rating Cafe Notes
10/10 Warda Patisserie Great vibes, fantastic coffee, great food options. In the midst of a collection of old church buildings called "The Village". Shepherd art gallery nearby.
9/10 Congregation Inside a beautiful church. Love the vibes and seating, good coffee. Many food/pastry options. Pizza place next door.
9/10 BasBlue — Midtown Beautiful building, great chai. Women's/non-binary club but first floor is public.
8.5/10 Saturn Coffee — New Center Cute place, bright. In-house made biscuit sandwiches that are delicious. Good coffee.
8.5/10 La Ventana — Eastern Market Great vibes and coffee, art on walls. Seating is nice and open.
8.5/10 Folk Detroit Always too busy, but great food options, great coffee.
8/10 Cafe Noir Good coffee; bright, inviting space. Cool art.
8/10 Trinosophes — Eastern Market Art and performance space. Great coffee, good vibes. Right by record store and Eastern Market. Breakfast sandwich comes with a nice freshly made salad.
8/10 Kresge Court Inside the DIA. Perfect vibes, coffee is great. Many snack/food options. Can be a little too busy/packed sometimes.
8/10 SPKRBOX Okay coffee but great vibes and setup. Love the cozy 2nd floor space. DJs here at night.
8/10 The Grand Porter Very nearby (SW Detroit by the high school). Good coffee, pleasant vibes. Food is pretty good and some vegan options.
8/10 Avalon Good coffee, great sandwiches, very slow service.
8/10 James Oliver — Corktown Decent vibes, great coffee, great bagel sandwiches.
8/10 Alba Great vibes, good coffee, some food options.
8/10 Seasons Market & Cafe Okay coffee, market has lots of great stuff including local produce, and pre-made meals from Baobab Fare. Cool upstairs seating area. Community garden on either side.
8/10 Moondog Cafe Live music, sells vinyls, good coffee.
8/10 Cafe Prince Nice space, great location, winery nearby, gym, bagels, magazine boutique. Grinds their own spices fresh for chai latte.
7.5/10 Cairo Coffee Really cool location inside book store / record store. Pourover coffee was good, which is all they have right now, but they'll have more soon. They do have cookies and baklava as well.
7.5/10 Washed Up Coffee Not a huge fan of the vibes, but another inside an old house. Great specialty coffee, rotating espresso which they will make into cappuccinos for you.
7.5/10 JP Makes and Bakes Mostly a Filipino bakery, but coffee is pretty good too. Not much space to sit down, more of a grab-and-go.
7.5/10 COMSA Coffee Honduran cafe that gets coffee direct from farmers. They really know their stuff, coffee was great. No espresso-based coffee at their food truck, they give 10% back to the urban garden they're in. At Cadillac Urban Gardens until 1 PM every Thursday.
7.5/10 Simply Roasted Nice clean modern vibes inside. Great customer service, customizable breakfast and lunch sandwiches. The breakfast sandwich on croissant was great, coffee is good.
7.5/10 Mad Nice Coffee Nice, clean, modern pastel interior. Coffee was good but not great; handmade sourdough bagels were very good.
7.5/10 Morningside Cafe Really cool black-owned art and music space, coffee and food items are great (and local).
7.5/10 Cafe Sous Terre — Midtown Great location and vibes (underground), coffee is okay.
7.5/10 Un Deux Trois Little coffee truck, great coffee, nice little outdoor area on Nick Gilbert Way.
7.5/10 The Roost — Woodbridge Decent coffee. Great barista, worked extra hard to make my specific drink. Smaller cafe but nice vibes and great outdoor seating.
7.5/10 Moka & Co — Garfield St Tasty Yemeni coffee. Very bright and open, good place to work during the day.
7.5/10 Decent Coffee Great coffee, nice vibes. Sketch location, great cardamom roll.
7.5/10 AWAKE Coffee Good coffee, relaxing vibe, lots of space. Very nice baristas.
7.5/10 Yellow Light — Central Station Good coffee, donuts, vegan option, coffee shake is tasty. Great if you're visiting the station but Alba is a better option in walking distance.
7/10 The Social Brews Coffee is good. Not a lot of bakery options. I love the fact that it's such a large open space and has a large patio. Not much of a view but still really nice. Has alcohol as well, kind of a sports bar with a coffee counter.
7/10 L'Artesano Mostly a juice/healthy wraps place, but the coffee is really good too and there is nice seating. It's usually pretty empty and not much to see where it's located though.
7/10 Vamonos Good drip coffee but no espresso still. Lots of good food options, healthy wraps. Vibes are so-so.
7/10 sml wrld Cute small space, vegan-only (non-dairy milks and vegan baked goods).
7/10 Anthology Old hipstery place, great coffee, vibes are a bit industrial.
7/10 Dose Detroit — Midtown Great coffee, good vibes, decent bakery options.
7/10 James Oliver — Guardian Building Great location to visit once for the building, coffee and bagels same as the other location.
7/10 Anna's Cafe Cute neighborhood spot to grab a quick coffee. Good chai latte.
7/10 Craig's Coffee Decent vibes, great coffee, in Chinatown. Nothing remarkable.
7/10 Cafecito Alvarez Cortado de olla was very tasty. No permanent location so can't judge vibes. Unique Latino-inspired drinks.
7/10 Detroit Sip Great coffee, kind of chocolatey. Decent vibes, nice sitting area outdoors across the street.
7/10 Qahwah — Dearborn Good coffee, bright vibes, plenty of space. Right by loop bagels.
7/10 Faust Haus — Eastern Market Coffee was good, drip only. It's just a stand and most of the items are flavored with syrups.
7/10 Haraz — Corktown Good coffee, tried their quishr. Lots of open space but a bit commercial. Good place to work.
7/10 Siren Cafe Decent coffee, love the Victorian decor. Small space.
7/10 Motor City Java House Relaxing vibes but not my cup of tea. Very sketchy area. They do have live music pretty frequently there. Coffee is okay.
7/10 Two Dudes Coffee Bar Protein lattes, vegan baked goods. Small, just a place to grab a coffee and go.
7/10 Cannelle Chai good but too sweet, great baked goods selection.
7/10 The Red Hook — Midtown Good coffee, nice healthy treats, good vibes.
7/10 Germack — Eastern Market Good coffee, a little commercialized, snacks and gifts.
7/10 Capper & Capper Great coffee, but just a little counter for the hotel nearby.
7/10 Dose Detroit — Fisher Great coffee, not a fan of the vibes.
7/10 Lucky Detroit Great vibes (two story old wood interior with barber shop attached), mediocre coffee, no food options.
7/10 Stella Good — Fisher Building Great vibes, good coffee, very empty though. Out of business.
6.5/10 Brush Park Bodega Really more of a small market/party store. The coffee was really good though despite being dark roast. Vibes were meh but it is in Brush Park so you can walk and see the nice old mansions or go down to Woodward.
6.5/10 SANA Cafe The coffee was really good, but super limited options and almost no seating. Also might be iced coffee only (no espresso machine?).
6.5/10 AM Buzz Coffee is decent, vibes are meh. Waited several minutes just to have my order taken, no-one at the counter. They have many different plant milks, at least.
6.5/10 Mitsos Greek Food & Coffee Might be better for just food. Coffee comes out of a machine, it's okay. Can't make non-dairy cappuccinos.
6.5/10 Water Square Cafe (Say Grace Collective) Good iced chai, right by the riverwalk / edge of centennial park. Small but nice seating, hotel cafe.
6.5/10 The Red Hook — Jefferson Coffee is good, nothing remarkable about the space.
6.5/10 The Red Hook — West Village Good coffee, small neighborhood space.
6.5/10 The Commons Cafe + laundromat. Coffee is okay, they have a vegan burrito if I stick around next time.
6.5/10 Rosa Campus Cafe — WSU Bookstore Often opens late (11 AM). Coffee was good, I do like the bright open space and of course you're in the middle of the WSU campus. Nice spot to sit down with your laptop and get work done.
6.5/10 Bar Rotunda Nice location in Book Tower, but coffee was meh and vibes were meh.
6.5/10 Rahha Yemeni Coffee & Brunch Good yemeni coffee, limited seating. Haven't tried their food, vibes are typical, bright interior for commercial yemeni coffee.
6.5/10 Shibam — Midtown Good coffee, some seating inside the Hilberry Gateway building. More of a grab-and-go spot for Yemeni coffee. They have sandwiches but I haven't tried them.
6.5/10 -320° Coffee & Creamery Good coffee, small place not really sit down, but right by Old Main.
6.5/10 Milwaukee Caffe Okay coffee, nice vibes, small neighborhood cafe.
6.5/10 Chai Khana Their Iraqi chai was meh. Seemed like no spices just sweetened black tea. Maybe their Qahwah or lattes are better. Bagel sandwich was also just okay, vibes are meh. Will try their other stuff later.
6.5/10 Madcap Detroit Modern, small, downtown in alley, busy. Coffee is good though.
6/10 CRED Cafe Coffee was decent, not a fan of the vibes, had a weird odor and location is a little iffy.
6/10 Sepia Coffee Smol, coffee isn't that great, location is meh.
6/10 Qargo Coffee is bitter. Very commercial space. Not for me.
6/10 Kitab — Midtown Doesn't even have middle eastern coffee. Sweetened spices lattes, typical stuff.
6/10 Kitab Cafe & Books — Hamtramck Nice spot, cool location, easy parking, all the books are Islamic, overly sweet coffee.
5/10 Roasting Plant Coffee Meh vibes, meh coffee.
5/10 Dessert Oasis Coffee is bland, vibes are meh.
