About

This website is a copy of an archive of a website that I made in FrontPage 4.0 in 2005-2006, when I was only 11-12 years old. It was originally hosted at 7thcolumn.net, by my mom's web design company, Foxxcomm, Inc. I have preserved the site as it was back then, including the original images, styling, and even the old-fashioned HTML code; but updated the text to give information on my house in Detroit. Please note that some links and images may not work, as not all assets were preserved. Enjoy this nostalgic glimpse into my early web design days!
